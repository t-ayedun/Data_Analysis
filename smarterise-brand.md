# Smarterise brand

Smarterise sells the reading of grid data. Deliverables should look measured and
unfussy: one accent, plenty of air, numbers that line up.

## Colours

Primary. `#053738` Green Energy · `#FFBA07` Sunlight · `#032B44` Cloud Power ·
`#F2FAEF` Clean Water

Secondary, support only, never leading. `#E3FC03` Glow Light · `#A1D3FF` Deep Sea ·
`#FF3333` Error · `#EAEAEA` Air Power · `#1D1D1D` Oil

Tints, 100/80/60/40/20, for fills and shading only, never for text:

- Green Energy `#053738` `#375F60` `#698788` `#9BAFAF` `#CDD7D7`
- Sunlight `#FFBA07` `#FFC839` `#FFD66A` `#FFE39C` `#FFF1CD`
- Cloud Power `#032B44` `#355569` `#68808F` `#9AAAB4` `#CDD5DA`
- Clean Water `#F2FAEF` `#F5FBF2` `#F7FCF5` `#FAFDF9` `#FCFEFC`

Rules that decide most questions:

- Green Energy is the default text colour and the default dark ground.
- Sunlight is the accent. One per layout, carrying roughly a tenth of the surface.
- Error red is a signal, not type. On white it measures 3.6:1 and fails for body
  text, so use it for dots, fills and icons and put the words in Green Energy or Oil.
- Text needs 4.5:1. On Green Energy or Cloud Power use Clean Water or white; on
  Sunlight, Glow Light, Deep Sea, Air Power or Clean Water use Green Energy, Cloud
  Power or Oil.

## Typeface

Outfit throughout, weight carrying the hierarchy. Fallback stack
`'Outfit', ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', sans-serif`.
On the web load it from Google Fonts. In Office the recipient must have it
installed; say so when handing over a file. Calibri is the substitute if a client
forbids an unfamiliar face.

Scale: display 800 at 56/1.02, slide title 800 at 40, section head 700 at 24,
body 400 at 17/1.55, emphasis 600 inline, caption 500 at 13, label 600 at 12
uppercase with 0.14em tracking. Running text near 65 characters. Tabular figures
wherever digits stack.

## Charts

Series order, every chart, every time: Sunlight, Cloud Power, Glow Light, Green
Energy, Deep Sea, Air Power, Oil. This maps to Office theme accents 1 to 6, so a
native chart in a Smarterise template is already correct.

Horizontal gridlines only, Air Power at 0.75pt. Axis labels 12pt Green Energy. No
title inside the plot, since the slide or section title says what the chart shows.
No legend on a single series. Never a chart pasted in as an image, never a
truncated bar axis, never more than six series.

## By output type

**Spreadsheets.** Header rows take a Sunlight band with Green Energy bold text.
Band alternate rows in Clean Water, never both banding and gridlines. Right align
figures with a consistent number format, name units in the header rather than the
cell, and mark cells someone should fill in with a Sunlight 20 `#FFF1CD` fill.
Total rows get a Green Energy rule above and below. Use named cell styles rather
than direct formatting, so the sheet restyles in one move later.

**Documents and reports.** Green Energy text on white, logo in the header, page
number in the footer. Tables follow the spreadsheet rules. A callout sits in
Sunlight 20, at most one a page.

**Decks.** Dark cover, light body, dark close, Sunlight dividers between chapters.
Five lines a slide. Layouts, logo placement and the corner icon come from the
master, so nobody places them by hand.

**Web and product.** Clean Water or white ground, Green Energy ink, Sunlight
accent. Cards 14px radius, chips 8px, pills 999px. Borders 1px in Green Energy 20.
Spacing in multiples of 8. Shadows almost never.

## Logo

Tile and mark are a colour pair and are never recoloured. Approved pairs: Sunlight
tile with Green Energy mark (the default), Sunlight with Cloud Power, Clean Water
with Green Energy, Oil with white, white with Oil.

Clear space on all sides equals the height of the tile. Minimum sizes: icon 24px,
horizontal lockup 120px wide, lockup with tagline 180px wide, below which drop the
tagline. Never stretch, rotate, add effects, place a lockup on a busy photograph
without a solid panel, or retype the wordmark in a font, since it is outlined
artwork.

**Old files are off-brand.** Anything with a `#F5C242` tile or a pure black mark
predates the 2025 revision. Flag it and replace it from the brand kit.

## Avoid

Accent stripes and rules under headings · centred body text · gradients ·
stock photography of engineers in hard hats · glowing blue circuit-board imagery ·
more than one accent competing in a layout · Sunlight used so widely it reads as a
warning.

## Verification

Before handing anything over:

1. Every colour used appears in the palette or tint list above. No near misses.
2. Text over any fill clears 4.5:1.
3. Chart series follow the fixed order.
4. The logo variant matches its ground, and no `#F5C242` or pure black mark survives.
5. Outfit is specified with the fallback stack, and Office files say the recipient
   needs it installed.
